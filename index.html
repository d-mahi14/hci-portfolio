ℹ️ How SUS scoring and benchmarks work (Click to expand)

The System Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996) calculates scores ranging from 0 to 100:

  • Odd questions (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) contribute: (Selected Score − 1)
  • Even questions (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) contribute: (5 − Selected Score)
  • Total score = (Sum of Contributions) × 2.5

Curved Grading Scale (Sauro & Lewis, 2016):
A+ (84.1–100) • A (80.8–84.0) • A- (78.9–80.7) • B+ (77.2–78.8) • B (74.1–77.1) • B- (72.6–74.0) • C+ (71.1–72.5) • C (65.0–71.0) • C- (62.7–64.9) • D (51.7–62.6) • F (0–51.6).

A score of 68 is the industry average (grade C), not 'above average'. Scores above 71 represent above-average usability.
